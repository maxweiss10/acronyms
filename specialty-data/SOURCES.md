# Sources

`emr-notes-meta-inventory.txt` and much of `note-clipped-words.txt` were curated from the gaps found by
comparing this dictionary with the abbreviation inventories in the **Medical Abbreviation and Acronym
Meta-Inventory** that were built from real medical records:

- Vanderbilt clinic notes and discharge summaries (semi-automatically derived from the medical record)
- Stetson et al., cross-cover sign-out notes, Columbia University Irving Medical Center
- Columbia obstetrics/gynecology clinical notes

Grossman Liu L, Grossman RH, Mitchell EG, et al. A deep database of medical abbreviations and acronyms
for natural language processing. *Sci Data* 8, 149 (2021). https://github.com/lisavirginia/clinical-abbreviations
Licensed CC BY 4.0. Entries were rewritten, filtered (institution names, physician initials, typos dropped)
and assigned specialties here; they are not a verbatim copy.
